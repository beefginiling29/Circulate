THE FOUR CHAMBERS

Heart anatomy

RIGHT ATRIUM

Receives deoxygenated blood

Blood returning from the body enters this chamber through the vena cava.

RIGHT VENTRICLE

Pumps toward the lungs

It pumps deoxygenated blood into the pulmonary circulation.

LEFT ATRIUM

Receives oxygenated blood

Oxygenated blood returning from the lungs enters through the pulmonary veins.

LEFT VENTRICLE

Pumps to the body

It pumps oxygenated blood into the systemic circulation through the aorta.

VESSELS

Know the pathway

VesselDirection / role
ArteriesCarry blood away from the heart.
VeinsCarry blood toward the heart.
Pulmonary arteriesCarry deoxygenated blood toward the lungs.
Pulmonary veinsCarry oxygenated blood from the lungs to the heart.
AortaCarries oxygenated blood from the left ventricle to the body.
Vena cavaBrings deoxygenated blood from the body to the right atrium.
